RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS
Business Insights Report
SQL Data Analytics Project
Tool Used: MySQL
Dataset: Sample Superstore

1. Executive Summary
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data using MySQL and identify important business insights related to sales performance, regional performance, product profitability, customer behavior, and yearly sales trends.
The analysis helps identify the strongest sales region, highest-performing product category, top customer, and loss-making products.
2. Key Business Findings
1. Regional Sales Performance
•	Finding: West region generated the highest sales.
•	Total Sales: $713,471
•	Insight: The West region is the leading sales contributor among all regions.
2. Category Revenue Analysis
•	Finding: Technology generated the highest revenue.
•	Total Revenue: $835,900
•	Insight: Technology is the top-performing category in terms of revenue.
3. Category Profit Analysis
•	Finding: Technology generated the highest profit.
•	Total Profit: $145,387
•	Insight: Technology is also the leading category in profitability.
4. Top Customer Analysis
•	Finding: Sean Miller is the top customer.
•	Total Sales: $25,043.05
•	Insight: Sean Miller contributed the highest sales among individual customers.
5. Loss-Making Product Analysis
•	Finding: Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print generated a loss.
•	Total Loss: -$8,879
•	Insight: This product requires further investigation into its pricing, discounting, and operating costs.
6. Yearly Sales Analysis
•	Finding: 2017 recorded the highest annual sales.
•	Total Sales: $724,994.56
•	Insight: 2017 was the strongest sales year in the dataset.
7. Overall Profitability Analysis
•	Finding: The overall profit margin was 12.45%.
•	Insight: The business generated approximately $12.45 in profit for every $100 in sales.
3. Business Recommendations
1.	Regional Strategy: Study the West region's sales performance and identify strategies that could be applied to other regions.
2.	Category Strategy: Continue focusing on Technology products to support revenue and profitability.
3.	Customer Strategy: Analyze the purchasing behavior of high-value customers and develop customer retention strategies.
4.	Product Strategy: Review the pricing, discounts, and costs of loss-making products.
5.	Sales Strategy: Investigate the factors behind the strong sales performance in 2017.
4. Conclusion
The SQL analysis identified the West region as the highest sales contributor and Technology as the leading category in both revenue and profit. Sean Miller was the top customer, while the Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print recorded a significant loss.
The business achieved an overall profit margin of 12.45%, and 2017 recorded the highest annual sales.
These findings provide useful insights that can support better business decisions, improve profitability, and strengthen sales performance.
